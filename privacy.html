English

1. Who to contact

2. Information and purposes

If you email or message us, we receive the details you send, such as your name, business email, phone number, country, product specifications, quantities, destination port and correspondence. We use them to answer questions, prepare quotations, coordinate samples and orders, and support customers.

The inquiry form prepares an email draft on your device. Preparing it does not send the inquiry; you must send it from your email app. Please include only information relevant to your business request. If you do not provide a way to contact you or sufficient specifications, we may be unable to respond or quote.

Where applicable data-protection law requires a legal basis, handling an individual's requested quotation may involve steps before a contract; communicating with business representatives and protecting our systems may involve legitimate business interests; required records may be kept to meet legal obligations. Optional marketing and analytics use your permission where required.

3. Optional product updates

You can request a quotation without subscribing to marketing. The optional email choice is unchecked by default and is included in the draft you choose to send. To stop product updates, reply asking to unsubscribe or email info@dingshengan.com. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing or necessary communication about your active request or order.

4. Website providers and analytics

GitHub Pages hosts this website. The homepage also loads fonts, a display script and some images from third-party providers, including Google Fonts, unpkg and Unsplash. These services receive connection information such as an IP address when the relevant resources load. Email services process messages you send; WhatsApp processes communications when you use its service. Their own privacy notices also apply to their services.

Google Analytics loads only after you accept optional analytics. It measures website use; the initial page measurement uses the page path without query parameters. We do not intentionally include inquiry field values in analytics events. You can reject analytics and still contact us. Choose to change your choice; withdrawing analytics permission reloads the page to stop its active code.

Your privacy preference is stored in this browser for up to 180 days, and your language choice is remembered in local storage. You may clear these using your browser settings. Google Analytics may set its own measurement cookies after permission; their expiry also depends on the service configuration. Third-party services may keep technical logs independently of optional analytics.

5. Recipients and international handling

Your request may be handled by our sales and support personnel and the service providers needed for communication. For orders, relevant details may also be provided to production, payment, shipping or professional service partners as needed, or disclosed when legally required. The website, email and communication services may process information in China or other countries. Contact us for details about the recipients and transfer arrangements applicable to your request.

6. Retention

Retention depends on the status of your inquiry, ongoing customer relationship, support needs, applicable business-record obligations and any disputes. We keep information only for the purposes and periods that remain necessary. Minimal records may be needed to respect an unsubscribe request. Contact us to ask about the retention applicable to your records.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a portable copy of your information. You may object to processing, particularly direct marketing, and withdraw optional consent. We may need to confirm your identity and assess record-retention requirements before acting. You may also complain to the relevant data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

8. Updates

We will update this notice when our handling changes, including before introducing new inquiry or marketing services. Material changes will be brought to your attention where appropriate. The date above identifies this version.

如适用法律要求说明处理依据，为个人准备其主动请求的报价可能属于订约前步骤；与企业代表沟通及保护系统可能基于正当业务利益；必要记录可能为履行法律义务而保存。可选营销及分析在需要时基于您的许可。

3. 自愿订阅

您无需订阅营销邮件即可询价。营销选项默认不勾选；您的选择会写入您决定发送的邮件草稿。如希望停止产品更新，可直接回复要求退订，或联系 info@dingshengan.com。撤回不影响此前合法处理，也不影响就您正在进行的询价或订单所需的沟通。

4. 网站服务商与分析

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。首页还从 Google Fonts、unpkg 和 Unsplash 等第三方加载字体、展示脚本或部分图片；加载资源时，相关服务商会收到 IP 地址等连接信息。邮件服务商处理您发送的邮件；您使用 WhatsApp 时，该平台也会处理沟通信息。相关服务同时适用各自的隐私说明。

只有在您同意可选分析后，Google Analytics 才会加载，用于了解网站使用情况。初始页面统计使用不含查询参数的页面路径；我们不会主动将询价表单内容写入分析事件。拒绝分析不影响联系。通过 可更改选择；撤回分析许可时页面会重新加载，以停止已运行的分析代码。

隐私选择在当前浏览器中最多记忆 180 天；语言选择存于本地存储。您可以在浏览器设置中清除。许可后，Google Analytics 可能设置统计 Cookie，其到期时间还取决于服务配置。第三方服务自身的技术日志与可选分析并不相同。

5. 接收方与跨境处理

销售及支持人员、沟通所需的服务商可能处理您的需求。处理订单时，也可能按需向生产、支付、物流或专业服务合作方提供相关信息，或依法律要求披露。网站、邮件及通信服务可能在中国或其他国家处理信息。如需了解适用于您请求的接收方及跨境安排，请联系我们。

6. 保存期限

保存时间取决于询盘状态、持续客户关系、支持需要、适用业务记录义务及争议情况。信息只在相关目的仍然必要的范围和期间内保存。为尊重退订选择，可能需要保留最少量记录。您可以询问自身记录适用的保存安排。

7. 您的选择与权利

依适用法律，您可能有权要求访问、更正、删除、限制处理或获取可携带副本，也可以反对相关处理，尤其是直接营销，并撤回可选许可。处理请求前，我们可能需要核实身份和必要的记录保存要求。您还可以向所在司法辖区的相关数据保护机关投诉。

8. 更新

处理方式变化时，包括引入新的询价或营销服务之前，我们将更新本政策，并在适当情况下提示重要变更。上方日期标明本版本。
