Dingshengan — Your Trusted Roofing Manufacturer in Foshan

Founded in 2016, Dingshengan is a professional manufacturer of ASA synthetic resin roof tiles and PVC corrugated roofing sheets. Located in Foshan City, Guangdong Province — the heart of China's building materials industry — we integrate R&D, production, and global sales.

Our modern factory is equipped with 8 automated co-extrusion lines and an in-house quality testing laboratory. With an annual output exceeding 50 million square meters, we serve distributors, contractors, and wholesalers worldwide across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, South America, and more.

What Makes Our Tiles Different

☀️

ASA Engineering Resin Surface

Imported ASA resin with UV stabilizers. Zero fading for 20+ years. Performs in -40°C to +80°C extreme temperatures without cracking or warping.

🔥

B1 Fire Retardant Grade

Officially certified to China B1 fire safety standard. Self-extinguishes within seconds of flame removal. No burning droplets — critical for fire safety.

⚡

1/6 the Weight of Clay Tiles

At just 6-8 kg/㎡, our tiles dramatically reduce roof structural load, shipping costs, and installation time. No cranes or heavy equipment needed.

💧

100% Waterproof & Corrosion-free

Zero water absorption. Impervious to acid rain, salt spray, and industrial chemicals. Ideal for coastal areas and chemical plant environments.

🔇

30dB+ Noise Reduction

Multi-layer composite structure absorbs rain impact noise. Tested to reduce noise by over 30 decibels vs metal roofing — noticeably quieter indoors.

🎨

Custom Colors & OEM Service

In-house color lab matches any RAL color. Custom thickness, length, and branded packaging. Your specs, your brand — we manufacture to order.
