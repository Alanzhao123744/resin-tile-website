Buyer Guide

Roofing Product & Ordering FAQ

Practical answers for distributors, contractors and project buyers.

What information is needed for a roofing quotation?

Send the product or profile, thickness, color, quantity, required sheet length, destination country or port, and any packaging requirements. Our sales team can then confirm the specification and prepare a quotation.

What is the difference between ASA resin tiles and UPVC corrugated sheets?

ASA synthetic resin tiles are commonly selected for villas, resorts, temples and residential projects where appearance matters. UPVC corrugated sheets are a lightweight option for factories, warehouses, farms and carports. The suitable profile depends on the building, climate and project specification.

Can roof sheet colors, thicknesses and lengths be customized?

Dingshengan supports custom colors, sizes and packaging. Available profiles, production tolerances and order requirements are confirmed by our sales team for each project.

Can I request roofing samples or technical specifications?

Yes. Send the product and profile you are evaluating, your destination and the tests or dimensions you need. Our sales team will confirm current sample availability, delivery cost and relevant technical documents.

Do you support export orders for distributors and contractors?

Yes. We work with distributors, wholesalers, contractors and project buyers. Contact our sales team to discuss packaging, trade terms, destination-port requirements and export documents for your order.

How can I contact Dingshengan sales?

Email info@dingshengan.com or contact us on WhatsApp at +852 9826 6341. Include your product, quantity and destination so we can respond efficiently.
