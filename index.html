Dingshengan — Foshan Roofing Manufacturer Since 2016

Founded in 2016 and located in Foshan City, Guangdong Province — the heart of China's building materials industry — Dingshengan specializes in ASA synthetic resin roof tiles and PVC corrugated roofing sheets. With 20 automated co-extrusion production lines, we deliver quality, consistency, and competitive pricing.

Our factory covers over 30,000㎡ with an in-house quality testing laboratory. Annual output exceeds 50 million square meters, serving distributors, contractors, and wholesalers across Southeast Asia, Middle East, Africa, and South America.
