Who We Are — Dingshengan Building Materials

Founded in 2010 and located in Foshan City — China's building materials capital — Dingshengan is a professional manufacturer specializing in ASA synthetic resin roof tiles and UPVC roofing sheets. We integrate R&D, production, sales, and after-sales service under one roof.

Our 30,000㎡ factory houses 10 fully-automated co-extrusion lines with an annual capacity exceeding 50 million square meters. We serve distributors, contractors, and wholesalers across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, South America, and beyond.

What Makes Our Resin Tiles Different

ASA Engineering Resin Surface

Imported ASA resin with UV stabilizers ensures zero fading and cracking for 20+ years. Performs in extreme temperatures from -40°C to +80°C.

B1 Fire Retardant Grade

Officially tested and certified to China B1 (difficult to ignite) fire safety standard. Self-extinguishes within seconds of flame removal. No burning droplets.

1/6 the Weight of Clay Tiles

At just 6-8 kg/㎡, our tiles dramatically reduce structural load, shipping costs, and installation time. No heavy equipment needed.

100% Waterproof & Corrosion-free

Zero water absorption. Unaffected by acid rain, salt spray, or industrial chemicals. Ideal for coastal, chemical plant, and high-humidity environments.

30dB+ Noise Reduction

Multi-layer composite structure absorbs rain impact noise. Significantly quieter than metal roofing — tested to reduce noise by over 30 decibels.

Custom Colors & OEM Service

In-house color matching lab. Any RAL color available. Custom thickness, length, and packaging. Your brand, your specifications — we manufacture to order.
